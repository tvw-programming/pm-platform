# Quick Start Guide

Get your team up and running in under 15 minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Create your workspace

When you first open the app you land on the workspace creation screen.

![Create workspace — step 1 of 4](/screenshots/onboarding-step1.svg)

Fill in three things:

- **Workspace name** — visible to every member (e.g. "Acme Engineering")
- **URL slug** — becomes `app.example.com/<slug>`, letters and hyphens only
- **Plan** — choose Free to start; you can upgrade later without losing data

Click **Continue**.

---

## Step 2 — Invite your team

![Invite team members — step 2 of 4](/screenshots/onboarding-invite.svg)

Type each email and press **Enter** to add them as chips. Choose a role for each invite:

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Admin** | Invite/remove members, change settings |
| **Member** | Create tasks, plan sprints, use chat |
| **Viewer** | Read-only — great for stakeholders |

You can skip this step and invite people later from **Settings → Members**.

---

## Step 3 — Create your first project

After onboarding, click **+ New Project** in the left sidebar. A project is a container for tasks, sprints, roadmap items, and chat threads. Most teams start with one project per product.

---

## Step 4 — Add tasks to the backlog

Open your project and go to **Backlog** (left sidebar). Click **+ Task** or press `⌘N`. Fill in:

- **Title** — short, action-oriented ("Add OAuth login")
- **Type** — Feature, Bug, Chore, or Spike
- **Priority** — P0 (critical) through P4 (someday)
- **Story points** — leave blank if you don't estimate yet

Add as many tasks as you have ideas. You can organize them into sprints next.

---

## Step 5 — Plan and start your first sprint

1. Open **Sprints** in the left sidebar
2. Click **+ Sprint** to create a sprint with a name and goal
3. Drag tasks from the Backlog panel into the sprint
4. Click **Start Sprint**, set the end date, and confirm

Your sprint is live. The **Active Sprint** board shows tasks in columns by status (To Do → In Progress → Done).

---

## Step 6 — Use chat for decisions

Open **Chat** in the left sidebar. Every message is threaded and searchable. For technical decisions that affect multiple roles, switch to **Event Mode** (the toggle in the composer) and select a playbook — this routes your message to the right people and creates trackable tickets.
